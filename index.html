How the rating works
  • Your cameras (up to 40 points): how often bucks showed up within about 2 hours of first or last legal light, including near misses just outside it. Sightings in the 72 hours after a feed drop are left out.
  • Recent activity (up to 20): buck visits in the last 7 days compared with the season's average. Days a camera sent no photos at all (dead batteries, no signal) are skipped, so they don't count as days with no bucks.
  • Weather (−25 to +40): a cold front (high 8°F or more below the day before) and frost help; highs of 60°F or more and rain chances of 70% or more hurt; the first dry day after heavy rain helps.
  • Wind: −5 when the wind during the sit reaches 13 mph, −10 at 20 mph, −3 for gusts of 30 mph or more.
  • Air pressure: +3 when it has risen 1.5 hPa or more in the 12 hours before the sit, +5 for 3 hPa or more (deer tend to move as a front clears).
  • Snow: +8 for the first snow of the season (half an inch or more in the last day, none in the month before), +5 for fresh snow, +2 for snow already on the ground.
  • Fog (mornings only): −5 if visibility at first light is under about a quarter mile, −2 if under a mile.
  • Does (up to 5, rut only): +5 when does at Cam002 in the last 3 days are 1½ times the usual. Starts once does have been counted for 10 days.
  • Moon: shown on each day but not scored. Your sit log will show whether it matters at your stand.
  • Time of year (up to 10): peak rut in early November scores highest, then late November and the second rut in early December.
  • Today's call uses the better of today's sits that haven't ended. Go is 65+, Worth a sit 45–64, Skip under 45.
  • A rule of thumb, not a prediction. The weather and each sit you log are saved, so the ratings can be checked against what you actually saw.
  • Wind, air pressure, fog and snow come from Open-Meteo, hour by hour for each sit.
  • Camera photos are read from Spypoint each morning. The forecast comes from the National Weather Service every 3 hours. Sun times are for the camera area. Legal light: ½ hour before sunrise to ½ hour after sunset.
  • People are found by looking through the photos (Spypoint's Human Activity tag is locked on your plan). Spypoint's Buck tag is automatic and can be wrong.
