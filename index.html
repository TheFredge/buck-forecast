How the rating works
  • Your cameras (up to 40 points): how often bucks showed up within about 2 hours of first or last legal light, including near misses just outside it. Sightings in the 72 hours after a feed drop are left out.
  • Recent activity (up to 20): buck visits in the last 7 days compared with the season's average.
  • Weather (−20 to +30): a cold front (high 8°F or more below the day before) and frost help; highs of 60°F or more and rain chances of 70% or more hurt; the first dry day after heavy rain helps.
  • Time of year (up to 10): peak rut in early November scores highest, then late November and the second rut in early December.
  • Today's call uses the better of today's sits that haven't ended. Go is 65+, Worth a sit 45–64, Skip under 45.
  • A rule of thumb, not a prediction. The weather is saved every day, so it can be checked against your cameras over time.
  • Camera photos are read from Spypoint each morning. The forecast comes from the National Weather Service every 3 hours. Sun times are for the camera area. Legal light: ½ hour before sunrise to ½ hour after sunset.
  • People are found by looking through the photos (Spypoint's Human Activity tag is locked on your plan). Spypoint's Buck tag is automatic and can be wrong.
